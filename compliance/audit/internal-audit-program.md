# Internal audit program

## TL;DR
Internal audits verify your own controls work *before* an external auditor (or attacker) finds the gaps. Required by ISO 27001 (Clause 9.2) and good practice everywhere. Independent, planned, evidence-based, and feeding corrective action.

## Purpose
```text
- Confirm controls are designed AND operating effectively.
- Catch gaps early; drive continual improvement (feeds management review).
- Prepare for external audits (ISO cert, SOC 2) — no surprises.
- Independent assurance to leadership and the board.
```

## Program elements
```text
1. Audit charter/scope – authority, independence, what's in scope (ISMS, controls, processes).
2. Risk-based plan     – audit high-risk/critical controls more often; annual schedule.
3. Auditor independence – auditors don't audit their own work.
4. Execution           – interviews, evidence sampling, control testing.
5. Findings & CAPA     – nonconformities -> corrective/preventive actions with owners + dates.
6. Reporting           – to management; track remediation to closure.
7. Follow-up           – verify findings are actually fixed.
```

## Conducting an audit
```text
- Plan: objective, scope, criteria (policy/standard), sampling approach.
- Fieldwork: gather evidence (see audit/evidence-checklist), test controls, note gaps.
- Classify findings: nonconformity (major/minor) vs observation/opportunity.
- Report: factual, tied to a control/requirement, with recommended remediation.
```

## Findings & corrective action (CAPA)
```text
- Root-cause analysis (not just the symptom).
- Corrective action (fix now) + preventive action (stop recurrence).
- Assign owner + due date; track to closure; verify effectiveness in follow-up.
```

## Best practices
```text
- Risk-based frequency; rotate scope so everything is covered over a cycle.
- Independence + competence of auditors; document methodology.
- Feed results into management review and the risk register (see risk-management).
- Automate evidence collection to make audits repeatable (GRC tooling).
```
